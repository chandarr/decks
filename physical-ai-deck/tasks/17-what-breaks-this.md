- **Rows (Q → A):**
  1. **"Why doesn't NVIDIA own this?"** → Not neutral — its stack locks you to its silicon; ours picks the best model + body + chip across vendors. And it has no appetite for the unglamorous assurance work. Firsthand: its edge hardware isn't deployment-reliable. Reliability is our ground, not theirs.
  2. **"How does it make money?"** → Services / forward-deployed now (US/EU) → orchestration licensing → assurance & certification fees. Cash early, platform later.

  4. **"Isn't this too much?"** → No — one beachhead, proven, then generalized. Near-term is focused; the rest is clearly a later tier.
  5. **"Can you attract world-class talent to India?"** → We recruit on a field worth owning (embodied assurance), a world-class core over a deep base, and global nodes. India is talent and proving ground — not the market.
- **Takeaway (central band):** **A leader is judged by the questions they've already asked themselves.**
