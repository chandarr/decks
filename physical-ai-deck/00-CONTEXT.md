# 00 — Context & Meta-Goals

## Project
HTML pitch deck — **"Winning the Widening Middle of Physical AI"** — a Head-of-Technology plan for a vendor-neutral, India-HQ'd (globally-scoped) Physical AI research + monetization lab positioned between frontier model labs (left) and robot/embodiment providers (right). Full strategy in `reference/Physical-AI-Lab_Strategy-Handoff.md` (v2).

## The real job of this deck (meta-goal — drives everything)
**Not** to convince the founder of the vision — he is already sold on the space. **The deck must convince him that AC is the right person to lead the technology.** The strategy is the *vehicle*; the *demonstrated qualities* are the payload: clarity of thought, structured thinking, presentation craft, deep domain expertise, and realistic execution vision. Success metric: **by the last slide, he wants to send an offer letter.**

## The audience (one person)
- **The founder.** Techno-commercial; took a large gaming/entertainment/network company to IPO. Sharp market watcher, clear on direction and working style.
- **Division of labour:** he owns market + funding; AC owns technology. They will work closely if this proceeds.
- **What earns his respect:** clarity, structure, foresight (anticipating hard questions), firsthand execution reality, honest de-risking. **What loses it:** vision hand-waving, hype, overselling, unfocused scope, claims he can puncture.
- **What he already believes:** the space is real and worth entering; he does not need a market/why-now sell. He needs to be *moved* on one thing only — that AC is the technical leader.

## The ask
No fixed commercial ask, and **no explicit ask for an offer letter.** The close should *imply* it — "what are you waiting for" — by making hiring AC feel like the obvious, inevitable next step: how we'd work together, what AC owns, first moves. Confident, not soliciting. Never a "fund this" pitch.

## Hard constraints / corrections
- **Geography:** India = talent base, R&D, and initial low-cost testing/proving ground. **Monetize in USA/EU** (India has limited robotics acceptance + price sensitivity). Do not position India as the primary market.
- **Neutrality:** vendor-agnostic in both model world and robot world — non-negotiable and load-bearing for the Composer.
- **Assume nothing** about the company's current assets (capital, hardware, IP, partnerships).
- **Partner, not compete** with frontier labs and robot OEMs — enabler framing, never extractor/zero-sum.

## Design intent
- Slide 1 = a full-thesis visual **map** (answer-first Minto), which **recurs as a you-are-here locator** at each act transition.
- High information density, but readable. HTML deck (deck-builder runtime).
- Register: senior, technical, confident, realist. No hype.
