## Copy

- **Kicker:** `What we build · 2 of 3`
- **Title:** **Autonomy — goals, not tasks; and a system that learns.**
- **Goals-not-tasks:** Hand the system an objective, not a script. It decomposes the goal into tasks and figures out how — task-and-motion planning married to a reasoning planner.
- **Tools & memory:** Its tools are SLAM, localization, perception, motion primitives; its memory is a spatial/world model that persists across a job.
- **The flywheel:** A human says *"gentler, and rotate first"* — and the policy updates, not just the plan. Language-conditioned correction folded into reasoning **and** motion. Every deployment compounds; the fleet is the asset.
- **Carry:** `teach it to fish — every correction improves the platform`
