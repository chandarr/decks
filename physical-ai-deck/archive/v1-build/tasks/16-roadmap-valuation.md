## Purpose

Sequence the plan so each milestone both shows visible progress AND re-rates valuation, and make the revenue mechanics explicit (weak joint #2). Market is US/EU; India is the proving ground.

The one idea: *Beachhead → buckets → Composer → standard-setter → environments — and value steps up at each inflection.*

Transforming horizontal timeline with five stages left→right, on an ink axis. Each stage: a label, a one-line milestone, its revenue mode, and a rising valuation step (an ascending ink stair beneath the axis — schematic, not a fabricated chart). A thin legend notes the stair is illustrative of *re-rating*, not forecast numbers. Kicker/title top-left. Carry bottom.

## Copy

- **Kicker:** `The roadmap`
- **Title:** **Each stage re-rates the company.**
- **Stages:**
  1. **Beachhead** — one superhuman vertical, delivered (US/EU); India proving ground. *Revenue: services / forward-deployed.* → proof + cash.

- **Carry:** `progress you can see — and a step-change in value at each stage`
