# Page 15 — IP strategy · "Publish the science, patent the methods, keep the data"

> Inherits `GLOBAL-INSTRUCTIONS.md`. Light theme. Act IV.

## Purpose

Show a deliberate IP posture that balances research credibility (which recruits) with defensibility (which compounds). Make clear what we open, what we protect, and why.

The one idea: *Publish to attract, patent the methods, and keep the failure-data and Composer recipes as the crown jewels.*

## Layout

A three-column matrix: **PUBLISH · PATENT · TRADE-SECRET**, each column listing the assets that belong there with a one-line rationale. A bottom band: "standards as position" (shaping certification is influence, not a filing). Kicker/title top-left. Carry bottom.

## Copy

- **Kicker:** `What we own`
- **Title:** **Publish the science. Patent the methods. Keep the data.**
- **Publish:** research on embodied assurance, world-model compression, and evaluation — credibility that recruits the best people and sets the field's terms.
- **Patent:** model→embodiment adaptation methods, edge-orchestration, and safety-case techniques — defensible, licensable.
- **Trade-secret:** the failure-data corpus, the Composer's validated recipes, and deployment data — the compounding crown jewels; never published, never patented.
- **Standards as position:** shaping the certification standard is influence, not a filing — a durable seat at the head of the table.
- **Carry:** `open what recruits, protect what compounds`
