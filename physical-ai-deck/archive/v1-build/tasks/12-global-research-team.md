# Page 12 — The global research team · "Why the best people come"

> Inherits `GLOBAL-INSTRUCTIONS.md`. Light theme. Act IV.

## Purpose

Answer the talent-draw scepticism honestly: how a world-class research team is sourced to an India base, and why top people join — the magnet is an under-served field they can own.

The one idea: *We recruit by offering a discipline to own, not a salary to beat.*

## Layout

Left 40%: kicker, title, the honest framing line. Right 60%: three "magnet" cards in a rail — Own a field · Global by design · Real deployment — each with a one-line substantiation; beneath, a partnerships strip (frontier labs, universities). Kicker/title top-left. Carry bottom.

## Copy

- **Kicker:** `How we build · talent`
- **Title:** **The best people come to own a field.**
- **Framing:** We don't recruit on cost of talent. We recruit on the chance to own an under-served discipline — embodied assurance and the composed middle — before anyone else defines it.
- **Cards:**
  - **Own a field** — embodied safety/assurance is wide open; a senior researcher can define it and be first.
  - **Global by design** — India HQ, remote research nodes, US/EU field presence; work with the frontier, not behind it.
  - **Real deployment** — models that touch the physical world, with a data flywheel most labs never get.
- **Partnerships:** frontier model labs (as partners, not competitors) · universities (India + global) · robot OEMs.
- **Carry:** `India's engineering depth + a field worth owning`
