# Page 17 — What breaks this · "The hard questions, pre-answered"

> Inherits `GLOBAL-INSTRUCTIONS.md`. Light theme. Act IV close / foresight climax.

## Purpose

Turn the founder's sharpest attacks into a display of foresight. Present the top hard questions with crisp, honest answers — the single most direct vehicle for the deck's real job (AC is the right leader).

The one idea: *I've already found where this breaks, and here's how I'd de-risk each one.*

## Layout

A two-column Q→A grid, five rows. Left cell = the hard question (mono, `--alert`-tinted marker). Right cell = the crisp answer (ink). A confident header line frames it as foresight, not defensiveness. Kicker/title top-left. Carry bottom.

## Steps (reveal order)

**0 — Base:** kicker, title, the framing line, empty grid rows.
**1–5:** each Q→A row reveals in turn (`fadeUp`), question then answer.
**6 — Close line:** carry.

*(6 beats. Presenter can also reveal all at once via End if pressed for time.)*

## Copy

- **Kicker:** `What breaks this`
- **Title:** **I've already found the weak joints.**
- **Framing:** The sharp questions, answered before you ask them.
- **Rows (Q → A):**
  1. **"Why doesn't NVIDIA own this?"** → Not neutral (locks to its silicon); no appetite for the unglamorous Assurance layer; firsthand, its edge hardware isn't deployment-reliable. Reliability is our ground, not theirs.
  2. **"How does it make money?"** → Services / forward-deployed now (US/EU) → Composer licensing → assurance/certification fees. Cash early, platform later.
  3. **"A startup as a certification body — really?"** → Standard-setter + safety-case tooling + failure-data first; partner with TÜV/UL; authority is earned, not claimed.
  4. **"Isn't the Composer hand-waving?"** → v1 is a human-in-the-loop config tool over validated recipes; autonomy grows with the data. Incremental, not magic.
  5. **"Superhuman is niche vs. a horizontal platform."** → Go vertical-deep on one beachhead, generalize the platform from it. The tension is real; the sequencing resolves it.
- **Carry:** `a leader is judged by the questions they've already asked themselves`
