## Copy

- **Kicker:** `What we build · 1 of 3`
- **Title:** **Adaptation — fit intelligence to the body and the job.**
- **Tiers:**
  - `DELIBERATIVE` — scene reasoning, task planning — *offloaded to cloud when connectivity allows*
  - `REACTIVE` — physics-aware policy — *on the edge, ~1–5 Hz*
  - `REFLEX` — balance, collision, compliant control — *deterministic, classical, milliseconds*
- **Cards:**
  - **Orchestration** — the framework that manages the split, the offline fallback, and hand-off latency. *The piece the field lacks.*
  - **Specialization** — distillation, transfer, task-specific tuning → small, fast, certifiable experts. A general model here is not just unnecessary, it's detrimental.
  - **Compression** — store perception in embedding/latent space, not human-viewable frames. The vision half of VLA/world-models has converged; freezing it is low-risk — and it's what makes "budget" real.
- **Point:** **The asset is the repeatable pipeline — generic model + application data → certified edge expert. We sell the factory, not the widget.**
- **Carry:** `the moat is the partition, not the model`
