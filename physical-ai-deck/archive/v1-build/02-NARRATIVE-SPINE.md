# 02 · Narrative spine

## The arc

The founder already believes in Physical AI and owns market + funding; he needs to believe AC is the technologist to lead it. So the deck moves him not from doubt-in-the-space to belief-in-the-space, but from *"sharp candidate"* to *"this is the person"* — by walking one coherent, pressure-tested thesis (the widening middle) from a single-picture answer, through the three capabilities and the emergent product, to a de-risked execution plan and a close that makes the offer feel inevitable. Tension: both ends racing, no one owns the enabling middle (and NVIDIA looks like it might). Resolution: the failure-data→certification moat + neutrality + India talent, sequenced to exponentiate valuation.

## Opening-page decision

**Concept chosen:** Convergence / thesis-map ("the Bridge") — two worlds (model/embodiment) as anchorages, three cables (Adaptation/Autonomy/Assurance), an ink keystone (the Composer), superhuman above, real-world application below. Answer-first Minto; recurs as the deck's you-are-here locator. Prototype approved (`reference/slide-01-thesis-map.html`).
**Alternatives considered:** the enabler-channel/flow (more abstract to read cold) and the Composer console (too product-specific to carry the whole thesis on page 1 — reserved for page 08).
