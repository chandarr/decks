# 00 · Context — KUKA India synopsis deck

## What this is
A screening synopsis, delivered as an HTML slide deck, routed through an advisor who asked to see the candidate's thinking before an introductory meeting with KUKA's Global CEO. The advisor reads it cold and alone first (self-standing copy required); the candidate later walks the CEO/advisor through it live (arrow-driven reveals). Fifteen screens, four acts: global trajectory → where KUKA sits → the state of KUKA India → the proposition.

## Target belief
"This person operates at global-strategy altitude, tells the truth about hard things, and sees a reframe of KUKA's India play that is bigger than a country-manager job — I want him in the room."

## Audience
**Primary:** the advisor (gatekeeper), judging rigor and range.
**Secondary:** KUKA Global CEO, Christoph Schell.
Both are technical and commercial evaluators. Design constraint: evidence discipline, honest limitations, restraint. The craft of the deck is itself part of the argument. Dark-throughout would read as "tech demo" and undercut credibility — hence light middle with dark bookends.

## The candidate
PhD robotics, 16+ years in robotics and AI, currently Head of Engineering & Product at a physical-AI / humanoid robotics company, based in Bengaluru. Positioning: the rare four-corner combination — robotics depth + physical-AI operating experience + India-insider knowledge + business framing.

## The ask (what happens next if this works)
An introductory meeting is granted, and the candidate converts an India-Head screening into a bid for a broader mandate touching global competitive strategy — on a trajectory toward a global role.

## The strategic thesis in one paragraph
KUKA's India entity is a ~76–100 person trader-support operation in the world's fastest-growing major robot market, while both principal rivals have already made their physical-AI moves *through* India — ABB carving out its robotics entity and hiring AI/ROS/simulation talent into Bengaluru and Pune, FANUC opening its architecture to ROS 2 and Python for Indian developers. KUKA has arguably the best software foundation of the three (iiQKA.OS rewritten from scratch as modular Linux) and the smallest army to exploit it. The proposition: rebuild India as both a deployment market and a physical-AI development base, funded by a self-standing turnaround of the existing business, and use the resulting data-and-deployment flywheel — Chinese manufacturing scale, Indian development cost, German precision, proven deployment expertise — as the engine of KUKA's global competitive position, replicable across Vietnam, Indonesia, Mexico and LATAM.
