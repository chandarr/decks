# Screen 09 — The paradox · "The market is not the problem"

> Inherits `GLOBAL-INSTRUCTIONS.md`. Global rules win unless named-overridden here.
> **Theme: light.** ACT III, opening. **This is the tension peak of the deck.** Density: high on both sides.
> **Deck is 15 screens** — the SWOT screen was dropped; screens 06–08 cover competitive positioning more sharply. Counters read `NN / 15` from here. See Notes.
> Source: `02-NARRATIVE-SPINE.md` screen 10.

## Purpose

Make the dashed corner from screen 08 concrete. The market side is not merely large and growing — India's stated manufacturing ambition is **structurally impossible without automation**, which makes the demand a precondition rather than a preference. The entity side is a trade-and-service operation without the capability to capture it.

The screen states both sides fully and connects them with a visibly broken channel. The panels themselves stay uncommented — nothing is written between them. Beneath the channel, one block names what the break is: the connective tissue that would carry demand into deployment is missing. **Added at the presenter's direction after the screen was built**, against this file's original no-commentary rule; the reasoning for that rule is in the Notes, and the block is written to keep as much of it as possible — it names the gap, it does not narrate the graphic or characterise the entity.

The one idea: *India's manufacturing ambition requires robots — and almost none of that demand currently reaches KUKA.*

## Steps (reveal order)

**0 — Base:** kicker, headline, both panel headings with their hairline rules, the flow channel rendered **complete and unbroken** in faint grey, all content hidden. The intact channel at base is deliberate — it is what the relationship *should* look like.

**1 — The market figures:** left block 1 `fadeUp`, figures `countUp` to `9,100 units`, `+7%`, `6th worldwide`.
**2 — The density gap:** the three density bars draw left-to-right in sequence (~200ms apart), India first and visibly shortest. Figures count up alongside.
**3 — The ambition:** left block 3 `fadeUp` — the four sector names, then the structural claim beneath, ~250ms later.
**4 — The break:** the flow channel animates. Strands travel left to right; the majority terminate at a break point roughly 60% across, their ends fraying into short dashes. **One thin strand continues through** to the right panel. The break is not violent — no shatter, no shake. It simply stops.
**5 — The entity:** right panel's five facts reveal in sequence (`fadeUp`, ~140ms apart). No counting, no bars, no orange — deliberately inert against the left panel's movement.
**6 — The gap statement:** the block beneath the channel `fadeUp`s — mono label, then the line, then the detail.
**7 — The closing line:** `fadeUp`.

*(8 beats. Back reverses one at a time. `R` replays from base.)*

**The break comes before the entity facts, not after.** It was built the other way round and moved: strands that stop short of a still-empty right panel state the disconnection more plainly than strands breaking against a panel the reader has already read. The facts then arrive as the account of that side.

## Data / graphics

**Density bars — hand-built SVG/CSS.** Horizontal bars, height ~14px, rounded ends. India `#FF5800`; world average and Germany `#C9C9CE`. Scaled to Germany = 100% width. The visual gap between India's bar and the other two is the runway argument and must be stark. Figures set in tabular mono at the bar ends.

**The flow channel — the screen's central device.**

- 7–9 strands, 1.5px, travelling left to right across the channel, gently curved and slightly irregular so they read as flow rather than as a diagram.
- Base state: all strands complete, `#E5E5E8`, spanning the full channel.
- At step 5: strands animate as flow (a subtle dash-offset drift, not a race), and **all but one terminate at a break point ~60% across**, their ends fraying into short dashes over ~20px.
- **One strand continues through**, thinner than the others, reaching the right panel. That single surviving strand is KUKA's current share of the demand, rendered without a number — the visual makes the claim proportionally without asserting a figure that would need sourcing.
- Break-point treatment: no jagged edge, no shatter, no shake. The strands simply stop. Restraint is essential; a dramatic break would read as editorialising and undercut the screen's neutrality.
- Colour: strands `#C9C9CE`; the surviving strand `#FF5800`.
