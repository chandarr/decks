## Purpose

Screen 07 asserted that KUKA is under-armed. This screen proves it in the one market the proposition depends on — and does so without reading as an attack on colleagues.

The headline number is headcount. The *diagnostic* row is composition: three companies in the same market have made three different bets about what an India organisation is for. ABB is building an engineering base. FANUC is building a deployment and developer engine. KUKA is running a channel.

The screen's protective move is the derived insight: **KUKA India is more revenue-efficient per head than ABB Robotics India.** That is not a defect — it is exactly what a well-run trading operation looks like. It sells efficiently and builds nothing. Stating it converts the critique from personal to structural, and demonstrates the author is reading the numbers rather than selecting them.

The one idea: *KUKA India is not underperforming its mandate. The mandate is the problem.*

## Notes

- **Why the derived insight is on the page.** A comparison showing only that KUKA is smallest reads as a case for the prosecution. One that shows KUKA is smallest *and* most revenue-efficient per head reads as a diagnosis. It is also the mechanism that keeps the critique structural: nobody is underperforming, the mandate is wrong. Do not cut it for space — it is the screen's ethical and rhetorical spine.
- **Why the method footnote is on the page.** The ABB headcount is the softest number in the deck. Disclosing the derivation on the rigor screen demonstrates analytical method and pre-empts the one challenge a well-informed reader could make. Rendering it as a bare number would be the single biggest credibility risk on this screen.
- **The POSTURE row is factual, not editorial.** `Selling and servicing imported product` is what the registry classification and the operating model describe. The parenthetical `(channel and service model)` names the model neutrally. No adjectives, no judgement of people, no reference to individuals, backgrounds or tenure anywhere on this screen — see `CLAUDE.md` content rules.
- **The HUBS row does quiet work and needs no commentary.** Two competitors are anchored in Bengaluru; KUKA is not. The locational argument is *made* on screen 11 (the diagnosis) — here the fact simply sits in the table and is allowed to be noticed.
- **Cross-screen dependencies:**
  - This screen quantifies the "under-armed" claim made qualitatively on **screen 07**. The word "army" does not appear here; the numbers do the work.
  - The Gurugram/Bengaluru consequence is developed on **screen 11**. Do not pre-empt it.
  - FANUC's `Opening the platform to Indian developers` is stated here as fact; its competitive consequence for KUKA's own developer play is held for **screen 14** (the ecosystem play).
- **Verification flags** (carry into `VERIFY-BEFORE-SENDING.md`): every figure on this screen needs confirmation before sending, and this is the screen where an error costs most. KUKA India revenue and the FY24 profit direction are third-party scrapes pending primary MCA verification. FANUC India headcount and revenue need cross-checking against Tracxn and MCA. The ABB derivation rests on the ~3.6% revenue-share figure and the ~5,027 total-staff figure — confirm both against ABB India's annual disclosures, since the whole estimate and its footnote depend on them.
