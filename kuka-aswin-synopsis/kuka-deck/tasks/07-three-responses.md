## Purpose

Screen 06 showed *that* the field is converging. This screen gives the cause, and converts the India case from "opportunity" to "existential."

Two groups are travelling toward the same point from opposite directions. The traditional leaders hold the body and are buying the brain. The humanoid and physical-AI companies hold the brain and are building or buying the body. KUKA is already standing at the intersection — certified sensitive hardware plus an operating system rewritten from scratch as a modular open platform — and has not yet moved.

The finding is an absence. The screen states that fact and does not explain it.

The one idea: *Both sides are converging on the position KUKA already occupies — and KUKA has not yet made its bet.*
