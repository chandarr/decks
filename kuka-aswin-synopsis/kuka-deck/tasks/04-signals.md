## Purpose

Screen 03 argued the technology is inflecting. This screen proves the *market has already priced it* — the money and the incumbents have moved, in public, on the record. It converts a technical thesis into a commercial fact pattern.

The structure carries an argument the numbers alone do not: three signals describe the industry, then four sub-boxes show **where the money has and has not gone** — priced at the brain twice (models alone, then embodied), discounted at the body, and stopped dead at certification.

The one idea: *This is not a forecast. Capital has already repriced the brain, still discounts the certified body, and nobody has crossed the certification line.*

**Revision, superseding the earlier three-sub-box version.** Figure is a hardware company, so using it as "the brain" blurred the argument. The pure-play software companies are the sharper evidence — valued in the billions with no hardware, no certification and no installed base.

**The India pivot card was removed** when the sub-boxes landed: the screen cannot carry three signal cards, a full-width pivot and a four-box row at 900px. India now first appears on screen 12 (the paradox). If the Act I → Act II handoff needs India named earlier, the ACT II divider is the place, not this screen.

The four boxes sit inside **one bounded highlight block** — heading, orange headline, the row, and the inference line beneath it — so they read as a single argument rather than four loose facts. The block is the screen's conclusion and the only element on it carrying a tinted field and orange type.

- **Top ~18%:** kicker, headline, subtitle.
- **Middle ~42%:** three equal cards, white panels, 1px `#E5E5E8` hairline border, generous internal padding. Each card: big figure (display, tabular) → hairline rule → what it is (body) → **the consequence** (body, secondary ink, italic-free) → evidence chip with source (mono, small).
- **Lower ~34%:** the bounded evidence block — `#F5F5F7` fill, 1px `#E5E5E8`, 14px radius. Inside it, top to bottom: mono heading (12.8px, 0.16em tracking, secondary), the headline in display weight **orange** (26.4px), the four sub-boxes, then the inference line in body weight **primary ink** (17.6px). Grey field behind white boxes is what makes the boundary legible without a heavy border.
- **The evidence and the reading of it must not carry equal weight:** the headline is the only orange type on the screen; the inference is ink, not accent.
- Four equal sub-boxes in **one row, never wrapping to 2+2**. White fill, 1px `#E5E5E8`, compact padding, figures ~55% the size of the upper signal cards' figures (23.8px against 43.2px) — they are the evidence under the signals, not four more signals. Each box: mono label → hairline rule → figure → body → evidence chip on the floor.
- **The box order is the argument and must not be rearranged:** two boxes of capital at the brain, one of discount at the body, one of the line nobody has crossed.
- **Bottom:** the closing line, mono, secondary, left; counter right.
- **Ratio adaptation:** the deck scales a fixed 1600×900 canvas uniformly (`fit.js`), so 1440×900 is the same composition at 0.9× — there is no separate breakpoint and no CSS media query anywhere in the deck. The spec's "reduce sub-box padding ~22% and body text ~8% at 1440×900" is satisfied by that uniform scale; if a future change makes the row genuinely tight, cut padding and body size before touching figure size, and let labels wrap to two lines.
