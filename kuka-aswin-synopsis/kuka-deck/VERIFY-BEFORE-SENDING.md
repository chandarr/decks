# Verify before sending — checklist

This deck is a rigor screen: the advisor's only read on your rigor is this artifact, so one catchable error costs more here than anywhere else. Confirm each item against a primary source before the deck leaves your hands. Every figure below lives in `data.js` and can be swapped without touching layout.

## Tier 1 — must verify (appear on screen as load-bearing claims)

- [ ] **KUKA India FY24: ~+57% revenue growth against ~46% profit decline** (screens 09, 10). From Tofler/Tracxn scrapes of MCA filings, not primary. Pull the actual MCA financials and confirm both figures and the ₹200–300cr band.
- [ ] **KUKA India headcount ~76–100** (screens 09, 10, 11). Tracxn, as of 31 Jul 2025. Confirm current; decide whether to present as a single figure or a range.
- [ ] **ABB Robotics India 350–500 employees** (screen 10). This is a *derived estimate* from a revenue-proportion calculation, not a disclosed number. Either state the method on screen or replace with a defensible range. Do not present as disclosed.
- [ ] **FANUC India 530–780+ employees, >₹1,000cr revenue** (screen 10). Cross-check against Tracxn and MCA filings.
- [ ] **FANUC India physical-AI specifics** (screens 06, 14) — the "Open Platform" launch, InBolt/Stream Motion deployments on Indian lines, Tier-2/Tier-3 SME strategy. Currently sourced to Instagram reels and LinkedIn posts. Either verify against FANUC India's own channels, or restate at the level that is solid: FANUC has opened its architecture to ROS 2/Python and is pushing physical-AI capability toward emerging-market SMEs.
- [ ] **KUKA group EBIT margin ~1.5%** (screen 07). From the global briefing, itself flagged for verification against KUKA's Annual Report 2025.
- [ ] **iiQKA.OS claims** (screens 06, 14) — the "first robotics platform to achieve IEC 62443-4-2 SL2" claim and launch dates come from an AI search summary, not primary sources. Verify on kuka.com or drop the "first-in-class" framing. This one matters: if the cybersecurity-first claim is wrong, it discredits the surrounding argument.

## Tier 2 — confirm the source and label as estimate

- [ ] **India 9,100 units 2024, +7%, 6th globally** (screens 03, 09). IFR World Robotics 2025 — solid, but re-check the exact wording.
- [ ] **India robot density ~30 vs ~162 global average** (screen 09). Confirm which measure (density varies by methodology from ~5–7 to ~30 depending on workforce base) and state it.
- [ ] **ABB→SoftBank $5.375B** (screens 03, 05, 06). Confirmed Oct 2025, targeted to close H2 2026 — check whether it has closed or terms changed.
- [ ] **Chinese domestic share 57%** (screen 03). IFR 2025 gives 57%; MIR Databank gives ~51.6% for 2024. Pick one source and cite it.
- [ ] **Robotics VC ~$27.6B in 2025** (screen 03). PitchBook; other sources differ substantially (Crunchbase ~$15B, CB Insights up to $40.7B). Label as estimate and name the source.
- [ ] **Cost arbitrage "one-fifth to one-third of West/Japan"** (screen 13). Composite estimate — keep as order-of-magnitude, never precise.
- [ ] **Pure-play software valuations — Physical Intelligence and Skild AI** (screen 05, sub-box 1). Deliberately carries **no figure**: `$B valuations` only. Private valuations move fast and a combined total invites a correction about which round and when. Confirm both are still independent and still unshipped in hardware; **do not add a number to make the box look like the others.**
- [ ] **Figure ~$39B valuation** (screen 05, sub-box 2). Reported valuation, mid-2025 — confirm the round and date, or soften to "reported at tens of billions".
- [ ] **Incumbent EBIT margins ~1–5%** (screen 05, sub-box 3). Sourced to company annual reports — confirm the specific companies and years behind the range, and that it is EBIT rather than EBITDA or operating margin.
- [ ] **Zero VLA systems certified for unsupervised industrial duty** (screens 04 and 05). One constant, two screens. This is the strongest claim on either screen and the easiest to be caught out on — re-check before sending, and keep the wording identical in both places.

## Sanity

- [ ] Open the deck **cold in a fresh browser**, as the advisor will — fonts load, arrows work, the overview button opens, nothing depends on your machine or your context.
