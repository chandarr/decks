## Purpose

Screen 11 named the mandate as the problem. This screen proposes the updated mandate — in two layers that are **additive, not alternative.**

Layer 1 recovers the existing addressable market and stands entirely on its own: it is justified even if physical AI stalled tomorrow, and it is arithmetic against competitors already on the board. Layer 2 is the net-new market physical AI unlocks, stacked on top.

The structural argument is the asymmetry: **Layer 1 funds and de-risks Layer 2.** The downside case is a fixed, competitive India business. The upside case is a global paradigm lead. A CEO is not being asked to bet on unproven technology — he is being asked to fix a subsidiary he should fix regardless, and to position it while doing so.

The one idea: *Take a bigger piece of the existing cake first. That pays for making the cake bigger.*

## Notes

- **Why the two-layer structure is the screen's job.** A single-layer "bet on physical AI" proposition is deferrable — a CEO can always wait for more evidence. A two-layer proposition where the first layer is self-justifying and funds the second is not deferrable in the same way, because declining it means declining a turnaround that stands on its own merits. The asymmetry is the persuasion; do not flatten it into one combined ask.
- **The arithmetic line is deliberately derived from screen 10, not from new research.** Both figures were already presented and chipped two screens earlier. Deriving a target from data the reader has already accepted is far stronger than introducing a market projection here.
- **RaaS is introduced here as a Layer 1 commercial mechanism only** — it converts capex resistance into an operating line and makes MSME and Tier-2/3 customers reachable. **Its second function — that owning the fleet turns every deployment into an instrumented data node — is revealed on screen 13.** Do not preview that here; the two-stage reveal is deliberate and it is what makes the flywheel land.
- **The Indian bank and NBFC financing point is substantive, not decorative.** Indian lenders are actively seeking new-economy asset classes, and structuring robot financing locally is a genuine unlock for capital-constrained MSMEs. Keep it as a named mechanism.
- **Cross-screen dependencies:**
  - The zone geometry is a direct callback to **screen 05**. Reuse the same axis orientation and relative proportions so the recognition is immediate. Consider sharing the geometry constants.
  - Layer 2's segment list is the payoff of screen 05's deliberately **unlabelled** dots. This is the screen where they get names.
  - `low-volume, high-mix` connects to screen 03's fourth contrast pair (`volume justifies automation → mix justifies automation`). Keep the terminology identical.
  - The geographic list (`Vietnam, Indonesia, Mexico, Brazil`) is previewed here in one line only and developed on **screen 14**. Do not expand it.
- **Verification flags** (carry into `VERIFY-BEFORE-SENDING.md`): the 3–4× multiple depends entirely on the KUKA and FANUC India revenue figures from screen 10, both of which are pending primary verification. If either changes materially, this line changes with it. Because it is derived rather than sourced, state the derivation if challenged rather than defending it as a finding.
