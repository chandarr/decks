# 02 · Narrative spine — 15 screens, 4 acts

Arc: Act I lifts to altitude before India is named. Act II proves competitive depth and locates KUKA honestly. Act III spends credibility on the truth about India (deliberate low point / tension). Act IV resolves into an expansive, earned bid. Tension peaks at screen 9, resolution at 13–15.

**Density principle (why 15 screens don't drag):** every screen carries three layers — a headline that lands in 3 seconds, a supporting visual that argues without prose, and a subordinate evidence layer (sourced specifics) that rewards the reader who leans in. A 3-minute skim reads as sharp strategy; a 10-minute study reads as deep research. Pacing alternates dense and light screens; no two data-heavy screens sit adjacent.

**Bookends:** screens 1 and 15 dark/cinematic (robot arm draws a trajectory). Screens 2–14 light and legible.

---

### ACT I — THE GLOBAL TRAJECTORY (screens 1–4)

**1 · Cover** *(dark)*
Animated 6-axis arm powers up joint-by-joint and draws a trajectory that resolves into the title.
Copy: "KUKA at the Physical-AI Inflection / A global synopsis — and a proposition from India."
Job: signal seniority and intent within five seconds. The arm executing a precise trajectory *is* the thesis rendered as motion.

**2 · The inflection**
Robotics is crossing from Automation 1.0 (deterministic, pre-programmed) to Automation 2.0 (intent-based, physical AI) — now, not eventually.
Job: establish the frame everything else sits inside. Light, single-idea screen.

**3 · The signals**
Four confirmed facts: ABB→SoftBank $5.375B; Chinese domestic majority at home; robotics VC roughly doubling; India #6 and fastest-growing.
Job: the money and the incumbents have already moved. Dense screen — counts up, evidence chips.

**4 · What physical AI actually changes**
Three mechanisms: reprogramming cost collapses → high-mix/low-volume becomes automatable; fixturing and safety-fencing fall away; the binding constraint becomes deployment data, not model architecture.
Job: prove technical depth to a technical CEO. This is where the deck stops looking like consulting slideware. Sets up the flywheel at screen 13.

### ACT II — WHERE KUKA SITS (screens 5–8)

**5 · The competitive map**
Big Four + Chinese OEMs + physical-AI startups plotted on hardware/certification depth × software-AI velocity. KUKA located honestly: strong vertical, exposed horizontal.
Job: show you can place KUKA in a field, not just describe it.

**6 · Three responses to one inflection** ← *strategic hinge of the deck*
ABB chose **ownership** (sold to SoftBank, carved out, re-staffed toward software). FANUC chose **alliances** (NVIDIA + Google/Intrinsic, then opened its architecture to ROS 2/Python). KUKA chose **architecture** (iiQKA.OS rewritten from scratch as modular Linux — arguably the better foundation).
Punchline, revealed last: **KUKA has the best platform and the smallest army to exploit it.**
Job: convert "opportunity" into "existential." This screen is the pivot of the whole argument.

**7 · SWOT as insight**
Four quadrants, each carrying the sharp point rather than the generic one — LBR sensitive robotics + iiQKA.OS as physical-AI-ready assets; margin and software-velocity exposure; the net-new market; commoditization and Chinese vertical integration.
Job: structured judgement, not a business-school grid.

**8 · The uncopyable combination**
The four corners — Chinese manufacturing scale and cost (via Midea) + India development cost + German precision and certification + decades of proven deployment. Each rival holds only some corners; KUKA-under-Midea can hold all four.
Job: the strategic keystone. Also quietly sets up the "why me" parallel at screen 15.

### ACT III — THE STATE OF KUKA INDIA (screens 9–11)

**9 · The paradox** ← *tension peak*
Split screen. Left: the market (9,100 units 2024, +7%, 6th globally, density ~30 vs ~162 world average, PLI/semiconductor/EV tailwinds). Right: KUKA India today (~76–100 staff, trader-support entity, revenue growth against sharply declining profit).
Job: the contradiction is the argument. No commentary needed.

**10 · The evidence — three-way India comparison**
KUKA vs ABB Robotics India vs FANUC India: headcount, revenue, hubs, and — the column that matters — **composition and posture**. ABB hiring AI/ROS/simulation into Bengaluru and Pune. FANUC field-application-weighted, opening its platform to Indian developers. KUKA channel-and-service, integration outsourced to third-party system integrators.
Second-order insight: KUKA is *more* revenue-efficient per head. That is what a well-run trading operation looks like — it sells efficiently and builds nothing.
Job: this is the rigor screen. Deep research made visible.

**11 · The structural diagnosis**
The entity was built correctly for a trader-support mandate; the mandate no longer matches the market. Includes the locational point: FANUC in Electronics City Bengaluru, ABB anchoring Bengaluru and Pune — KUKA in Gurugram and Pune, present in the automotive belt, **absent from India's deep-tech talent capital.**
Framed structurally, never personally. Generous to the people, damning to the design.
Job: credibility screen. The honesty here earns everything in Act IV.

### ACT IV — THE PROPOSITION (screens 12–15)

**12 · Two additive layers**
Layer 1: recover the existing addressable market (turnaround stands alone, self-funding, de-risks everything). Layer 2: the net-new market physical AI unlocks, stacked on top. Not "abandon the core to chase the new."
Job: make the bid approvable by asymmetry — downside is a fixed India business, upside is a global paradigm lead.

**13 · The flywheel** ← *centerpiece visual*
India development cost + diverse deployment (RaaS-funded) → the cheapest manipulation-data engine on earth → fine-tuned models on certified KUKA hardware → premium margin in West/Japan/Americas. RaaS is the mechanism: owning the fleet turns every deployment into an instrumented data node.
Timing beat: physical AI sits roughly where language models sat three years ago; the winners were positioned *before* capability arrived. In a data-compounding business, being second by two years is being second forever.
Job: the idea that earns the bigger mandate.

**14 · The ecosystem play**
Two moves that make it real fast: (a) a Bengaluru engineering footprint — the fix prescribed by screen 11; (b) partnerships with India's software-first robotics startups (CynLr-class vision and manipulation intelligence) built on iiQKA.OS's open architecture. KUKA supplies the certified body and the open OS; partners supply frontier perception. Faster than building in-house, and exactly what the architecture was designed for.
Closing-window beat: FANUC opening ROS 2/Python to Indian developers is precisely the move that pre-empts this. If a rival owns the developer relationship first, the better architecture stops mattering.
Job: turn vision into an executable, time-boxed plan.

**15 · The proposition, and why me** *(dark close)*
India as the proving ground for a global thesis; a mandate broader than country management; the global trajectory earned on results, not requested up front. Soft Midea line framed as opportunity — combining Chinese manufacturing strength with Indian development and German precision. Close on the four-corner parallel: the same rare combination in the candidate that KUKA needs to hold its own four corners. Arm trajectory resolves one final time.
Job: deliver the Phase-1 ask — "I want him in the room."
